# Investor guide

## Thesis

Signal & State is a service enabled software company for founder led B2B software companies between roughly two and thirty million in recurring revenue.

That company has customer data in four systems, a customer success team of one to ten, no customer success operations leader, and no reliable renewal forecast. It cannot yet justify a CS Ops hire, and it is exactly where an unnoticed renewal risk is most expensive.

## Model

1. **Customer Intelligence Sprint.** Fixed scope, creates initial revenue and proves the value on the customer's own data.
2. **Managed Customer Intelligence.** Recurring service revenue, operating the function while the team is still small.
3. **Ground Control.** Reduces delivery effort per engagement. Work compounds into the product rather than evaporating into a deliverable.
4. **Subscription.** As the product absorbs more of the delivery, the service component shrinks and the software becomes the primary contract.
5. **Partner delivery.** Eventually, other practitioners deliver on the same platform.

Consulting funds the build and supplies the requirements. The software makes the consulting repeatable and progressively cheaper. Each engagement makes the next one better.

## Why now

Customer intelligence tooling is either enterprise priced and enterprise complex, or a dashboard that reports what happened. The segment in the middle has the data and neither the operations capacity nor the budget for the enterprise answer.

## Status

Live in production with a public demonstration. Fourteen fictional accounts, deterministic scoring, eleven operational risk rules, a sixteen section executive brief, multi-tenant isolation tested against the production database, and an AI layer that works with no provider configured.

Pre-revenue. No customers yet.

## Deliberately absent

No market size claim, no revenue projection, no comparison to a funded competitor's numbers. None could be substantiated, and publishing one would undermine the credibility of everything else on the site.
