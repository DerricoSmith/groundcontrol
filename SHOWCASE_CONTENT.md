## Screenshots

Five, from the fictional demo, generated by `e2e/screenshots.spec.ts`. Each carries descriptive alternative text and links into the corresponding live demo page. Served through `next/image` so they are optimized and responsive.

## Rules the copy follows

- No em dashes.
- No banned marketing words.
- No fake logos, testimonials, results, or metrics.
- No unsupported market size or revenue claims.
- No compliance claim that has not been earned.
- Claude Code is described as part of an AI assisted workflow, never as the author.
- Every capability claim is checkable in the live demo.
