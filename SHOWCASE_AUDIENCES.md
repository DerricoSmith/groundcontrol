# Showcase audiences

One page, four readers. Each needs a different thing in the first thirty seconds.

| Audience | What they want | Where it is | The moment that lands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Recruiter or hiring manager | Is this person senior, and did they actually build this | Hero, 01, 04, 12, 13 | Section 12, particularly that three scoring decisions came from finding the opposite behaviour in his own product |
| Investor or partner | Is there a business here | 02, 14, 15, 16 | Section 14, the service enabled model, with no invented market size |
| Prospective client | Will this work for my company, and what is missing | 03, 04, 06, 11, 15, 16 | The live demo, plus the roadmap being explicit about what is not built |
| Technical evaluator | Is this real engineering | 06, 08, 09, 10 | Deterministic numbers with AI restricted to language, and the RLS status stated honestly rather than claimed |

## Ordering

Story before proof before commerce. Why it exists, what it does, what it looks like, how it decides, who built it, what it demonstrates, what it could become, what works, what does not.

A reader who stops after the walkthrough has still seen the product. A reader who stops after section 12 has the recruiter answer. Nobody has to reach the end.

## Guidance

- Send recruiters to `/showcase`.
- Send prospects to `/demo` and let the showcase be the follow-up.
