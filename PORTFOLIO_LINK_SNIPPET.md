## Recruiter description

Ground Control is a multi-tenant customer intelligence product I designed and built. It scores customer health from imported evidence across adoption, relationship, support, and commercial signals, detects renewal risk with eleven deterministic rules, and explains every number it produces. I made the product, data model, and scoring decisions myself and advanced the implementation through AI assisted development. It demonstrates executive customer success leadership alongside hands on product, data, and AI implementation work.

## Investor description

Signal & State is a service enabled software company for founder-led B2B software companies between roughly two and thirty million in recurring revenue. Consulting engagements deliver the customer intelligence outcome today, and Ground Control makes that delivery repeatable and progressively cheaper. The product is live with a public demonstration environment, and the initial offers span a fixed scope sprint, a managed service, transformation advisory, fractional executive work, and a software pilot.

## Prospective client description

Ground Control brings your customer, revenue, usage, support, and relationship data together so you can see which accounts need attention and why. It starts with a CSV export of what you already have, not an integration project. Every health score decomposes into components with their own confidence, every risk carries the evidence behind it, and where information is missing the product says so instead of guessing. Open the live demo and judge it in about two minutes.
