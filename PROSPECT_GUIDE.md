# Prospect guide

For a company evaluating Ground Control.

## What you can import today

CSV export from the systems you already use.

| Data | What it enables |
| --- | --- |
| Customer accounts | The portfolio, revenue, ownership, segments |
| Contacts | Champion and executive sponsor coverage, relationship scoring |
| Renewals | Renewal center, forecast confidence, milestone plans |
| Product usage | Adoption scoring, usage decline detection |
| Support tickets | Support experience scoring, unresolved priority detection |
| Customer interactions | Engagement recency, executive engagement, sentiment as recorded |

No integration project. No data warehouse. No professional services engagement before the first insight.

## What it identifies today

- A health score per account, decomposed into five weighted components with the records behind each
- Eleven deterministic risk rules covering adoption, relationship, support, and commercial signals
- Renewal exposure, preparation state, and an explained forecast confidence category
- Data quality issues and data freshness, including what cannot be assessed and why
- Suggested actions carrying the evidence from the risk that produced them
- A sixteen section executive brief

## What stays manual

- Getting the data out of your systems. Export is yours; import is ours.
- Deciding whether a detected risk is real. The product explains; you judge.
- Talking to your customers. Ground Control drafts internally and sends nothing.
- Anything needing billing data. Payment risk is not available.
- Goal tracking and success plans, so business outcomes scoring is inert.

## The pilot

1. Create your organization
2. Complete Quick Start
3. Import customer accounts
4. Review data readiness, which tells you what your data can and cannot support
5. Activate the health model
6. Review the first insight
7. Open the executive brief

A founding pilot can reach its first portfolio view during the initial setup session.

## Planned, not built

Native integrations for CRM, support, billing, and product analytics. Automated brief delivery by email and Slack. Validated predictive renewal modelling. SOC 2 certification.

All listed at `/trust`, which publishes limitations rather than hiding them.
